# Instep #3 — State Fair Parade

**Posted August 4, 2026**

The State Fair Parade will begin at 2:00 pm. The parade features the high school marching band exhibition, floats, animals, specialty units, and visiting royalty. The 14-block parade route begins near the Haunted House, goes north on Cosgrove Street, west on Randall Avenue, and south on Underwood Street, ending just south of the Haunted House. Students get free admission into the State Fair on this day. See the [parade route](https://www.irondalebands.org/uploads/4/8/3/5/48357505/instep_3_-_state_fair_parade_2026.pdf) on page 3 of the source PDF.

## Event Details

- **When:** Friday, August 28, 2026
- **Report:** 7:45 am at Irondale High School
- **Attire:** Irondale Show Shirt, Black Athletic Shorts, Athletic Socks & Shoes
- **What to Bring:** Instrument or Flag, $$ for Food/Games/Rides

## Notes for Parents

A permission slip is included on page 2 of the [source PDF](https://www.irondalebands.org/uploads/4/8/3/5/48357505/instep_3_-_state_fair_parade_2026.pdf) for students who wish to remain at the State Fair after the parade instead of riding the bus back to Irondale. Students remaining at the fair will not be supervised by band staff after the performance and must secure their own ride home; they're also responsible for their equipment, either by having a friend bring it back to Irondale or storing it in a parent vehicle after the parade.
