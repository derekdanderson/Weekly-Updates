# Instep #9 — Bandapalooza

**Posted October 8, 2026**

Bandapalooza is an annual event where our 8th grade students visit Irondale to perform with us during halftime of the home football game. This is about having FUN and getting to know the 8th grade students.

## Event Details

- **When:** Friday, October 9, 2026
- **Venue:** [Irondale High School](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=2425+Long+Lake+Rd%2C+New+Brighton%2C+MN+55112) (2425 Long Lake Rd, New Brighton, MN 55112)
- **Attire:** Fresh Air show shirt and black shorts. Middle school students are welcome to wear their Fresh Air shirt too — they will get a Bandapalooza shirt no matter what.

## Bandapalooza Itinerary

| Time | Activity |
|------|----------|
| 2:25 pm | Middle school dismissal |
| 2:45 pm | M.S. students bus to Irondale |
| 3:00 pm | M.S. students arrive at Irondale; welcome from Dr. L (Auditorium) |
| 3:30 pm | IHSMK students report to Irondale Band Room; meet & greet M.S. students (by section) |
| 4:00 pm | Practice halftime performance (Practice Field – grass): Part 4 of Fresh Air |
| 4:30 pm | Sectionals (captains and seniors lead practices to work on the songs + pep tunes) |
| 5:00 pm | Dinner; pass out 8th grade Bandapalooza shirts (provided by boosters) |
| 5:45 pm | Organize & move to stands |
| 6:00 pm | Kick-off |
| Halftime | Halftime performance |

_All students are dismissed after halftime, but students may remain in the stands with their families afterwards._

## Notes for Parents

- Students should plan to wear their Fresh Air show shirt and black shorts on Friday for Bandapalooza.
- If your student does not go to Irondale, please have them arrive at their earliest convenience.
- Dinner is provided to all students (IHSMK and 8th grade students) this evening.
