# Instep #2 — Mounds View Parade

**Posted July 29, 2026**

The Mounds View Parade is a part of the Mounds View Festival in the Park. Each year the Irondale High School Marching Knights participate in the parade.

## Event Details

- **When:** Friday, August 21, 2026
- **Drop-Off:** 5:15 pm at [Redeemed Life Ministries](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=7901+Red+Oak+Dr%2C+Mounds+View%2C+MN) (7901 Red Oak Dr, Mounds View, MN)
- **Pick-Up:** [Mounds View Community Center](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=5394+Edgewood+Dr%2C+Mounds+View%2C+MN) (5394 Edgewood Dr, Mounds View, MN)
- **Attire:** Show Shirt, Black Shorts, Athletic Shoes/Socks
- **What to Bring:** Instrument (leave cases in your car) or Flag/Rifle only
