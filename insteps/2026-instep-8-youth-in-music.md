## Meals

Lunch, dinner, and a late-night snack will be provided to students on this day. Students will receive a snack after finals. Lunch will occur on the bus as we drive to the show. We will serve students dinner after the awards ceremony. Length of dinner time is dependent on two factors: (1) if we make finals and (2) our finals performance time. The concessions stands only accept debit and credit cards.

## Bag Policy / Cameras

Youth in Music will be following all policies of U.S. Bank Stadium. NO CAMERAS will be allowed on field level. YIM is within the 24-hour window of an NFL game so magnetrons and National Game Day Security Protocol will be in place. [Bag Policy](https://www.vikings.com/stadium/bag-policy)

## Video Cameras / Copyright

Video cameras will not be allowed in U.S. Bank Stadium. YIM will be recording the event. Through the ASCAP/BMI license YIM is allowed to give out one copy to each performing unit. Performing units found duplicating their video performance or placing them online will be turned over to copyright lawyers for prosecution. YIM is under advisement of an entertainment and copyright lawyer. You have gained copyright to arrange the music, but not copy nor distribute your copy of the show. It is for educational purposes only. **If it is found on your website, played on local cable programming, on social media, YouTube or any other platform, it will be reported and your band/school/district will be responsible for all liability in copyright compliance.**
