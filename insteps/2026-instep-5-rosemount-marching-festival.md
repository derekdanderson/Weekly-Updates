## Competition Details

Bands are divided into Class A, Class AA, and Class AAA, with Irondale competing in Class AAA. All bands perform in the preliminary round of competition. At the conclusion of prelims, caption awards and class placement awards will be announced during the Prelims Awards Ceremony. At the end of the Prelims Award Ceremony, the top ten scoring bands, regardless of class, will be announced in random order. Those ten highest-scoring bands are invited to perform a second time in the evening finals competition. The finals competition will not include any classes but be run as an "open class" competition.

The Rosemount Marching Band Competition finalist performance schedule will be finalized based on a pre-draw conducted at the Band Check-in process prior to Preliminary Performances. The top 5 scoring bands from prelims will be assigned the last 5 performance times in numerical order according to the pre-draw. The next 5 scoring bands will be assigned the first 5 performance times in numerical order according to the pre-draw.

## Other Details (to Help You Be Successful)

- Go to bed early the night before.
- Eat breakfast before our morning rehearsal.
- Don't forget your long black socks, marching shoes, and compression wear along with your competitive uniform. You will hang your uniform on one of the z-racks located outside of the music suite before you head to rehearsal.
- To be early is to be on time. Arrive to school early enough so you can START on time. If you move slowly in the morning, arrive earlier to prepare for that.
- Drink plenty of water throughout the day.
- If you are someone who needs to graze, pack snacks and eat them during break times. Do not eat in uniform without wearing layers.
- If you feel physically regular, you will perform your best. Think about what you need to do to make this a GREAT day.
- When we are at Rosemount, be everyone's #1 fan. They are kids just like you working really hard at home to create their best show possible. Be supportive!

## Spectator Etiquette for Families

- Support every band. Every band is just high school kids like us!
- When you hear or see something that wows you, applaud! Bands feed off crowd energy.
- Be mindful of your comments in the stands. There are parents from every school sitting around you during the competition. Stay positive!
- Do not enter or exit the stands in the middle of a show. Always wait until the transition time in between bands to move.
- If you choose to record the performance, do NOT post it on social media until the season concludes on October 11, 2026. We want every audience member and judge to experience our show live for the first time.
- We don't compete for a trophy or a placement. We compete to push ourselves and receive feedback from the best adjudicators in the country. Focus on the growth over time rather than a singular moment in time. The only competition that truly matters is the one with ourselves.
