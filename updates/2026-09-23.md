## Bands of America Cedar Falls Trip

The Bands of America Cedar Falls Trip is Friday October 2nd – Sunday October 4th. All Mounds View Schools students will be excused from school on Friday October 2nd. If your student does not attend a school within our district, parents are responsible for excusing your student.

All students will need to bring $$ for our lunch stop at local mall in Rochester, MN on Friday October 2nd. Additionally, I highly recommend that students bring their own snacks to eat on the bus, especially if they are the kind of kid that is always hungry….like me! Students can bring soft drinks and energy drinks on the bus so long as they are closed containers (bottles are fine, but no cans that cannot be sealed after opened).
