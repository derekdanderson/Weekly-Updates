# Irondale Marching Knights — Weekly Update

**October 7, 2026**

## Final Weekly Update

With this being the final week of Marching Band, this is our FINAL weekly update of the season! Please take some time to read everything thoroughly so you know how each event works. Thank you for GREAT season. You should be so proud of your students.

Bandapalooza is an annual event where our 8th grade students visit Irondale to perform with us during halftime. This year's Bandapalooza is this Friday October 9th during the Home Football Game at 6pm. ## Youth in Music Championships

Our final competition is this Saturday October 10th at U.S. Bank Stadium! Please check the website for the In-Step and read all the spectator details for this specific show.

## Final Football Game

Our final football game will take place Wednesday October 14th at 6:00pm. The plan is to perform our full competitive show during Halftime one last time. This is the only football game where we will get to do so!

## Uniform & Instrument Turn-In

Uniform Turn-In will take place in the Band Room on Wednesday October 14th after our Football Game Halftime Performance. Students can keep their shoes and gloves as well as any compression gear they provided. We need the unitard, uniform top, and marching pants returned to us. All marching instruments will also be returned. Percussion instruments will move back up to the band room to be prepped for concert band and Winter Drumline.

## Yearbook Night

Every year the marching band students receive a marching band yearbook as a parting gift from the band program. The yearbook includes pictures students have taken throughout the marching band season. Yearbook Night is when we come back together to create our yearbook pages. See below for more details!

- Yearbook Night is scheduled for Thursday October 22nd at 6:30pm. Yearbook Night can last anywhere from 60-90 minutes, although many students will come and go throughout the evening.
- Yearbook Night is optional, although highly recommended. It's always a fun evening!
- Students are responsible for printing section pictures from the season prior to coming to Yearbook Night – these can be from the website or pictures from their phone. If students cannot print their own pictures, they can ask someone to print them via Student Slack.
- Snacks are provided this evening!

## Awards Night

Awards Night is our final event of the Marching Season and will take place on Monday November 9th at 6:30pm at Irondale High School. During this night each student will receive a special awards packet. We will also give out some superlative awards, watch the marching band show, and spend one last evening together before the season concludes. I will send a post in both the Student Slack and Parent Slack with more details about this evening at the end of October!
