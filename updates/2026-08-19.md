## 2026 Media Day

Our incredible band parent and photographer, Jill Peterson, is offering her first Marching Band Media Day! Media Day is going to take place on Friday September 11th from 3:30 – 5:00pm in the Irondale Cafeteria.

Media Day is a modern style of photography for teams/groups that focuses on bold lighting, dynamic posing, individual personalities, and action inspired looks. Participants are free to make their own poses or choose from available suggestions. This is an optional activity for our marching knights this year. Proceeds raised from the event are donated back to the band program, and students will wear their marching uniform.

Sign ups are available for this event and capacity is limited. Click [here](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1PGo0R9NehYFejRd0DJOxbf3BQZMyFu2d/view?usp=sharing) to view the flyer, which includes pricing and the link to sign up!
