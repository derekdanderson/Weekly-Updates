## Home Show Parent Volunteers

Home Show volunteer shifts are now open for registration! Parents, your volunteer efforts are what make our show a huge success! Volunteer Shifts range from facility clean up and decorating Friday evening to working a shift to help bands on to the field, serving up treats at concessions, or shuttling guests on our parking team. Shifts range from 90 minutes to 4 hours. If you can't work a shift, consider bringing a food item to support our hospitality lounge for bus drivers and coaches or for sales at our concessions booth. Training is provided for all shifts. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact Pam Anderson at [pam_s65@hotmail.com](mailto:pam_s65@hotmail.com) or 651-245-9601.

## Black Compression Undergarments

This is a reminder that all hornline and percussion members are required to provide their own black compression short-sleeved shirt and black compression shorts. The compression wear should be all-black and be skintight. This allows for a professional look. You can find suitable compression wear online and at many retail stores. Please make sure your student has their compression wear before the Eastview Marching Festival. Required Color Guard undergarments will be determined once they have the opportunity to try on the uniform.

## Hair Color for Competitions

During the competitive season, we ask that all performers sport a natural hair color from Saturday September 12th – Saturday October 10th. Because we do not wear shakos (marching band hats) as part of our uniform, our hair becomes part of the overall aesthetic. It is our hope that we bring our audiences into a completely different world, and so how we style our hair and apply our make-up can bring a huge amount of effect to the production. We will have more specific hairstyles and make-up guidelines next week. For now, know that your student will have options that work for lots of styles of hair!
