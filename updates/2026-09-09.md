# Irondale Marching Knights — Weekly Update

**September 9, 2026**

## Home Show Parent Volunteers

Home Show volunteer shifts are now open for registration! Parents, your volunteer efforts are what make our show a huge success! Shifts range from 90 minutes to 4 hours. If you can't work a shift, consider bringing a food item to support our hospitality lounge for bus drivers and coaches or for sales at our concessions booth. Training is provided for all shifts. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact Pam Anderson at [pam_s65@hotmail.com](mailto:pam_s65@hotmail.com) or 651-245-9601.

## Football Game Friday September 11

Our first official performance of "Fresh Air" will be at our Home Football Game on Friday September 11th. The student report time is 5:00pm, with kick-off beginning at 6:00pm. We will perform our competitive show at the end of the football game. The performance attire for this show is:

- Hornline & Percussion: Show Shirt, Black Shorts, Marching Shoes, Short Black Socks
- Guard: Rehearsal Black Clothes

Parents, if you plan to enter the stadium anytime during the football game, you will be required to buy a ticket. If you would like to enter for free, you must wait until the football game ends before you enter the stadium. At the conclusion of the game the gates will open, and you will be able to enter. You will have plenty of time to find your seats, as the football team must come off the field before we can move onto the turf to set up for the show.

## Black Compression Undergarments

This is a reminder that all hornline and percussion members are required to provide their own black compression short-sleeved shirt and black compression shorts. The compression wear should be all-black and be skintight. This allows for a professional look. You can find suitable compression wear online and at many retail stores. Please make sure your student has their compression wear before the Eastview Marching Festival. Required Color Guard undergarments will be determined after they try on the uniform.
