The Rosemount Marching Festival is this Saturday September 19th. This competition features two rounds: prelims and finals. The top scoring bands from prelims will advance to the evening finals round. Our prelims performance time is scheduled for 4:30pm. You can find additional show info below:

Admission Prices: (purchased at the gate)

- $15 Adults (single prelims ticket or single finals ticket)
- $10 Students (K-12) and Senior Citizens (single prelims ticket or single finals ticket)
- $25 Adults Combined Prelims and Finals Tickets (will get you into both events)
- $17 Students (K-12) and Senior Citizens Combined Prelims & Finals Tickets (will get you into both events)
